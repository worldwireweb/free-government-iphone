How to Qualify for a Free Samsung Phone

The government recognizes that communication is essential for safety and economic stability. That is why programs have been established to ensure that low-income individuals and families have access to essential telecommunications services. To qualify for a free government Samsung phone, you typically need to meet one of two main criteria: income-based eligibility or program-based eligibility.

1. Program-Based Eligibility

This is often the easiest way to qualify. If you or someone in your household already participates in one of the following federal assistance programs, you are automatically eligible:

  • SNAP (Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program): Also known as Food Stamps.
  • Medicaid / Medi-Cal: State-provided health insurance for low-income individuals.
  • SSI (Supplemental Security Income): Federal income supplement program funded by general tax revenues.
  • Federal Public Housing Assistance (FPHA): Section 8 housing vouchers or public housing.
  • Veterans Pension and Survivors Benefit: Support for wartime veterans and their families.
  • Tribal Assistance Programs: Including Bureau of Indian Affairs General Assistance, Tribally-Administered Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TTANF), Food Distribution Program on Indian Reservations (FDPIR), and Head Start (if income eligibility criteria are met).

2. Income-Based Eligibility

If you do not participate in any of the programs listed above, you may still qualify based on your household income. Generally, if your total household income is at or below 135% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines, you are eligible. This threshold varies by state and household size, so it is important to check the specific guidelines for your area.

Application Process Explained

Applying is simple. Once you have determined your eligibility, you will need to choose a participating wireless provider that offers Samsung devices in your state. You will then complete an application form, which will require you to upload proof of identity (such as a driver's license or passport) and proof of eligibility (such as a benefit award letter or tax return). Once your application is verified by the National Verifier, your free phone will be shipped directly to your doorstep.

Is the phone really free?

Yes, if you qualify for the Lifeline program, the device and monthly service are typically provided at $0 cost to you.

Can I keep my current number?

Most Lifeline providers allow you to port your existing phone number when you sign up for their service.
